Tests & Procedures Library

A comprehensive educational reference covering cancer screening, laboratory investigations, imaging, endoscopy, sampling, biopsies, pathology, molecular and genetic testing, staging investigations and follow-up procedures.

A listed test is not automatically required for every patient. The appropriate investigations depend on the suspected or confirmed cancer, symptoms, clinical findings, stage, treatment plan and specialist assessment.

Medical information notice: This library is for education and preparation, not diagnosis or personalised treatment advice. Tests and procedures should be selected, performed and interpreted by qualified healthcare professionals. Availability and terminology may vary between countries and healthcare systems.

Master Tests & Procedures Library

Cancer diagnosis and evaluation can involve several different types of investigations. This master library organizes the major test and procedure families used across oncology.

Individual cancer guides will progressively explain which investigations may be relevant to particular cancers, why they may be used, what they generally involve and how results are interpreted.
