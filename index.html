CRUSTAR CARE / RISK CHECK

Let's understand what you need next

This educational tool does not diagnose cancer. Select the statements that apply to you today.

1. Are you experiencing any new or persistent symptoms?

2. Are you concerned about cancer screening?

3. Do you have personal or family cancer history?

4. Have you already received a cancer diagnosis?

5. Are you having a medical emergency right now?

CRUSTAR CARE provides educational information and navigation support. It is not a substitute for examination, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

THE PURPOSE BEHIND OUR JOURNEY

In Memory Of

Er. Abdul Hai Khan

“May this work help another family find clarity, support and hope when they need it most.”

CRUSTAR CARE is being built in loving memory of Er. Abdul Hai Khan, with the hope of helping people navigate cancer with better information, compassionate support and greater dignity.

Important medical information: CRUSTAR CARE is an educational and support initiative. Its information and planned tools do not diagnose cancer, replace medical advice, or prescribe treatment. Seek assessment from a qualified healthcare professional for symptoms or concerns. For a medical emergency, contact local emergency services.
