Tests & Procedures Library

A comprehensive educational reference for cancer screening, laboratory investigations, imaging, endoscopy, sampling, biopsies, pathology, molecular and genetic testing, staging and follow-up procedures.

Now interactive: click any test or procedure below to see a brief explanation of what it is, how it is performed and why it may be used.

Medical information notice: This library is for education and preparation, not diagnosis or personalised treatment advice. A listed test is not automatically required for every patient. Availability and terminology may vary between countries and healthcare systems.
